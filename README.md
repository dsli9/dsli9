### Hi there 👋

Welcome to my page!

I'm Derek, a data engineer based in New York City who likes building data systems that actually get used.
Most of my work has focused on owning data platforms end to end, from ingestion to modeling to serving. 
I’ve built batch and event-driven pipelines, analytics warehouses in Redshift, and lakehouse architectures with Iceberg, all on AWS using infrastructure as code.

I’ve worked at startups and growth-stage companies where I’ve owned critical data systems, improved reliability, and helped reduce costs. 
I care a lot about keeping data platforms simple, scalable, and genuinely useful for both engineers and business teams.
